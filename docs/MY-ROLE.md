# My role in Go-Lewah

**Collins Nji**
Founder | AI-Assisted Product Development & Technical Documentation Lead
Go-Lewah Educational Platform

## Background

I have more than eight years of experience in technical communication, structured documentation, content
operations and technical support. I hold an M.A. in Technical Communication and eLearning from the University of
Limerick, Ireland.

Go-Lewah is where that background meets product development. Building a platform with AI assistance calls for the
same skills as good technical communication, applied at product scale:

- clear, structured specifications that leave no room for misreading;
- information architecture that people can navigate;
- precise rules for who may do what;
- careful review of every result against what was asked.

## My contributions

- **Conceived Go-Lewah** and defined its product vision and requirements.
- **Designed** the information architecture, user roles, permission models and functional workflows.
- **Produced detailed specifications** for school administration, academic management, the marketplace and the
  Radio features.
- **Directed AI-assisted development workflows** using Claude and other development tools.
- **Reviewed implementation results**, identified defects and coordinated iterative improvements.
- **Oversaw documentation, usability, bilingual English/French requirements and quality assurance.**
- **Managed development priorities and GitHub workflows.**

## How my professional skills show in this project

| Skill | In Go-Lewah |
|---|---|
| **AI-assisted technical communication** | Each development phase starts from a written brief: scope, constraints, acceptance criteria and the report I expect back. The AI tools implement it. I review the plan, the implementation report and the test results before approving the next step. |
| **Information architecture** | Separate portals for administrators, teachers, students, parents and vendors. Navigation grouped by school structure, people, teaching and learning. Dashboards ordered by what each role needs first. |
| **Structured content** | Ten standard radio-programme templates with timed segments. Reusable status vocabularies (submitted → under review → approved → scheduled). Every interface text available in English and French. |
| **Permission models and workflows** | Role-based access with delegated duties. Approve-then-publish for exam results. Request → review → approve → broadcast for radio programmes. Programme-specific rights that expire when the programme ends. |
| **Workflow automation** | Approvals, notifications, recurring schedules with per-episode changes, and automated test suites that verify every phase before it is accepted. |
| **Product documentation** | Specifications, governance texts, draft policies for legal review, and phase completion reports that separate what is implemented, what is prepared and what is not yet working. See the [documentation samples](samples/). |
| **Quality assurance** | Test scenarios and acceptance checks for permissions, consent, school isolation, bilingual screens and mobile layouts, verified by API, browser and real-audio tests ([testing approach](samples/testing-approach.md)). |
| **Risk control** | Development branch only. Production unchanged until launch approval. Credentials never exposed. Child protection and parental consent treated as non-negotiable requirements. |
